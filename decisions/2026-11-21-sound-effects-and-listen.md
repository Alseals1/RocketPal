# Sound effects and Listen everywhere

Date: 2026-11-21. Decided by the Plan agent for issue #34 (plan written 2026-11-13) and built by the Implement agent. Items 9, 10 and 15 are small changes made during the build. A person can overrule any line at PR review. The code is in `src/data/sounds.ts`, `src/core/cues.ts`, `src/app/sound.ts`, `src/platform/audio.ts`, `src/core/speaker.ts`, `src/audio/speech.ts`, `src/ui/listen.ts` and `tools/make-sounds.ts`. The types are in `docs/TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` sections 4.4 and 4.15, and the system in 5.12.

## What was decided
1. **Original, synthesised sounds.** Ten short sounds (boost, capsule, dot-found, docking, right, not-quite, star, rank-up, patch, bump) are made by a committed script, `tools/make-sounds.ts`, from sine and triangle tones and a little seeded noise. Soft attacks (5-60 ms), smooth decays, peaks at most -6 dBFS, and "not quite" is two quiet falling notes, never a buzzer. No samples or downloads, so there is no licence to approve (`CREDITS.md` row `sounds`). The script gives the same bytes every run, and a unit test checks the committed files against it.
2. **WAV files, mono, 16-bit, 22,050 Hz.** 238,582 bytes for all ten, under the 500 KB budget (`SOUND_TOTAL_BUDGET_BYTES`, tested). Each has a manifest entry of type `sound` with credit `sounds`.
3. **`SoundDef` and `Channel` move to `src/data/sounds.ts`** (the content layer), unchanged, so `Content.sounds` (4.2) can be typed there. `src/platform/audio.ts` re-exports them, so 4.15's names still work. A bad sound entry is left out on its own; with no `sounds.json` the game is silent and logs one error, like any other optional data file.
4. **Howler and the files load after the first tap or key.** Once Howler is in, the `AudioManager` makes one Howl per sound with `preload: true`. Before the first gesture nothing is fetched and nothing plays. A file that fails warns once ("Sound is off: a sound file could not load.") and that sound stays silent.
5. **One new event, `answered { place, correct }`**, emitted by the arrival screen when the quiz panel reports a pick. It is for sound and feedback only; the recorder ignores it.
6. **Reward sounds come from the progression diff, not from new events.** `ProgressionWiring.onRecorded(fn)` reports each recorded event with `diffProgression(before, after)`. `rewardCue` picks rank-up, then patch, then star; when it gives a sound it plays instead of the event's own, so one event makes one sound (the third dot on Mars plays "star", not "dot-found"). New events for star, rank-up and patch would be a second source of truth that could disagree with the diff.
7. **Only `src/app/sound.ts` calls `audio.play`.** It subscribes to the non-recorded cued events on the bus (`boost`, `answered`, `bump`, `capsuleCollected`) and to `onRecorded` for the rest. The same sound within 150 ms plays once (a kid tapping boost fast). `capsuleCollected` and `bump` are cued and tested now; #50 and #51 emit them.
8. **Visual partners.** Boost has the flame, dot-found the fact card, docking the landing, right and not-quite the ✓/✗ mark and words, star the star row. Rank-up and patch get a small toast here ("Rank up! You are a Junior Pilot now." / "You earned a new patch!"), using the existing toast component, until #46's reward screen replaces them.
9. **`Speaker` moves to `src/core/speaker.ts` (build change).** The plan had `src/ui/listen.ts` import `Speaker` from `src/audio/speech.ts` "as a type only", but the architecture test counts type imports and the plan also forbade new allow-list entries. The interface now lives in the logic layer, as `Settings` already does, and `speech.ts` re-exports it. This removed the two allow-list entries `ui/quizPanel` → `audio/speech` and `ui/surfaceHud` → `audio/speech`.
10. **The settings card scrolls inside the screen, with Done pinned at its bottom (build change).** With two more groups the card is taller than a phone held sideways at Extra big text. The card now has `max-height: 100%` and scrolls, and Done sits in a sticky footer on the card's colour, so it is always on screen.
11. **Settings levels, not sliders.** Sound effects: Off, Quiet, Medium, Loud (0, 0.25, 0.5, 1). Reading voice: Quiet, Medium, Loud (0.25, 0.5, 1); no Off, because Listen only reads when tapped and Off would just make it look broken. Any stored number from 0 to 1 still loads; the panel shows the nearest level, ties to the louder (the old default 0.8 shows as Loud). No save or settings format change. Effects Off is the mute.
12. **The reading voice volume reaches the speaker.** `Speaker.setVolume(v)` sets each utterance's `volume`; the game applies `settings.volume.voice` at start and on every change.
13. **Listen everywhere that exists today, through one helper.** `bindListen(button, speaker, text, { iconLabel? })`: a tap reads, a second tap stops, starting another Listen stops this one and puts its button back (the speaker calls the old `onEnd`). A text button says "No voice on this computer" and is disabled when there is no voice; an icon button hides. It is on Orbit's bubble, the mission card and the landed card. The `Patch` component takes an optional `onListen`. Opening a fact card stops Orbit or the mission reading.
14. **Listen on the reward screen (#46), the Journal (#53) and the patch board (#54) is left to those issues**, because the screens do not exist yet. They should use `bindListen` from #34.
15. **Where the two new Listen buttons sit (build change).** Beside Orbit's bubble, the button made the words wrap and Orbit grow about 47 px, which pushed into the mission card on a 320 x 568 phone. Orbit's Listen now sits above Orbit's face (beside it on a short landscape screen), so the words keep their full width. The mission Listen floats at the top right of the card; on screens 700 px wide or less, where the card already sits under the buttons, it goes in the free space just above the card's top-left corner, level with the settings gear. Both are solid navy with a cyan edge, like Orbit's bubble, so they show on a bright sky. The existing overlap tests at every size and text size pass.

## Why
- The game had read-aloud only on fact cards and the quiz, and no sound effects. Sounds that are data, cued from events, keep game code free of `play` calls and let a sound change without touching code. Original synthesised sounds avoid a licence question that would stop the issue.

## Alternatives
- **Openly licensed sound packs** (for example CC0): the issue says ask first, which would stop the issue for a licence call.
- **MP3 or OGG:** no encoder is installed, and adding one is a new dependency or a software install. WAV fits the budget.
- **Web Audio synthesis at run time (no files):** goes around Howler, `sounds.json` and the asset manifest that 4.15 and 5.12 specify.
- **New `GameEvent`s for star, rank-up and patch:** the diff already says this.
- **The quiz UI calling `audio.play`:** breaks 5.12, and UI may not import platform.
- **Range sliders for volume:** a fine-motor control on a touch screen; named choices are easier for 8-10 year olds and match the panel.
- **A two-column settings card on short screens:** less scrolling, but the sticky Done works at every size and text size with one rule.
