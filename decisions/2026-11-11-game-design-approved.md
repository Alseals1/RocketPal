# Rocket Pal 2.0 game design approved

Date: 2026-11-11. Decided by the product owner, who approved all eight recommendations in `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` section 19.

## What was decided
1. Progress has three kinds: stars (mastery), XP (effort, drives 8 ranks) and patches (achievements). There are no coins and nothing is spent.
2. The Grand Tour chapters suggest a route but never lock a world.
3. The asteroid belt is a new place on the Star Map, home of the Asteroid Belt Run.
4. The Journal and patches, and sound effects, are in the MVP. Music stays in Phase 2.
5. Call signs are picked from a list. There is no free-text input anywhere.
6. Gear lets you see and learn more but never makes a challenge easier.
7. The astronaut and its customization are Phase 2, because they need a new Blender model.
8. Fly-by worlds get one postcard question, not a 3-question quiz.

## Why
They keep the game inside the product rules (earned, visible rewards; nothing lost; no personal data) and keep the MVP buildable: the first content and modelling costs go to the loop, not to extras.

## Alternatives
Recorded with each section of `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`. The main ones: a coin shop (rejected, it adds spending and loss), locking worlds by chapter (rejected, it takes away free exploration), the astronaut in MVP (deferred, it needs a model first).
